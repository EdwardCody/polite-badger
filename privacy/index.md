# Polite Badger Privacy Policy

_Last updated: November 7, 2026_

Polite Badger shows your calendar, reminders and habits together on iPhone, iPad and Mac. It's built so that your information stays with you: **there is no Polite Badger account, no Polite Badger server, no analytics, no advertising and no tracking.** The developer never receives, sees or stores your data.

## What Polite Badger stores, and where

| Information | Where it's kept |
|---|---|
| Calendar events and reminders | In Apple's Calendar and Reminders, and in whichever accounts you already use with them (for example iCloud, Google or Exchange). Polite Badger reads and edits them through Apple's system access and doesn't keep its own copy. |
| Habits, habit history, day notes and energy check-ins | On your device. If iCloud is on, also in **your own private iCloud database**, so they appear on your other devices. Only you can read that database. |
| Diary entries | **Only on your device.** Polite Badger never syncs them to iCloud or sends them anywhere. Like all app data, they're included in your device backup if you back up your device. |
| Information from Apple Health | **Only on your device.** Polite Badger never copies Health data to iCloud or sends it anywhere. Like all app data, what it keeps is included in your device backup. |
| Settings and preferences | On your device. |

## Permissions Polite Badger asks for

Polite Badger asks for each permission only when you use the feature that needs it, and you can change your mind at any time in Settings.

- **Calendars and Reminders.** Used to show, create, change and complete your events and reminders.
- **Health** (iPhone and iPad). If you allow it, Polite Badger reads steps, exercise and mindful minutes, sleep, workouts, your Activity rings, and the nutrition you log (calories, protein, carbs, fat, water and caffeine). It uses them to complete the habits you connect to Health, compare them with your energy check-ins, and add them to your diary. If you turn it on, it also saves your energy check-ins to Health as State of Mind. Health information is never used for advertising and is never shared with anyone. It leaves your device only if you export or share it yourself (see below).
- **Location** (while the app is open). Used to show the weather and how long it takes to get to your events, and to set a reminder for where you are now. To answer, your location (and the addresses of your events) is sent to Apple's weather and maps services. Polite Badger doesn't keep your location, except a place you choose for a reminder, which is saved with that reminder in Reminders.
- **Notifications.** Used for the habit reminders you set. They are scheduled on your device.
- **Microphone and speech recognition.** Used only when you dictate a request to the assistant. Your speech is turned into text on your device.
- **Camera** (iPhone and iPad). Used only when you scan your work calendar. The photo is read on your device to find that day's meetings, and it isn't kept. The meetings are added to a calendar you choose, and only after you review them.

## Apple Intelligence

The assistant, the automated diary and Scan Work Calendar use Apple Intelligence. They run on Apple's on-device model, so your information is processed on your device.

## Sharing

Polite Badger doesn't sell, rent or share your information with anyone. It contains no third-party code for advertising, analytics or crash reporting.

Information leaves your device only in these ways, all under your control:
- iCloud, which syncs to your private database;
- your calendar accounts, which sync your events and reminders;
- Apple's weather and maps services, which receive your location and event addresses when you view the weather, maps or travel times;
- anything you export or share yourself, which goes wherever you choose to send it.

## Exporting and deleting your data

In Settings you can export your habits, day notes, check-ins and diary entries (including any Health readings in them) as a file, and delete all of your habits, notes, check-ins or diary entries. Deleting them from one device also removes them from iCloud and your other devices. Deleting the app removes everything it stored on that device. Your events and reminders stay in Apple's Calendar and Reminders, where you manage them as usual.

## Children

Polite Badger isn't directed at children and doesn't knowingly collect information from anyone.

## Changes to this policy

If this policy changes, the new version will be posted here with a new date. Polite Badger will never start collecting your information without asking you first.
