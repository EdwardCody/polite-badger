# Polite Badger

Your Apple Calendar events, Apple Reminders and habits in one calm view of your day, on iPhone, iPad and Mac.

There is no account to create, no server and no tracking. Your events and reminders stay in Apple's Calendar and Reminders, and everything else stays on your devices and in your own private iCloud.
