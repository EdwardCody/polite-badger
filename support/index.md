# Polite Badger Support

Email **[roper_filmy.8q@icloud.com](mailto:roper_filmy.8q@icloud.com)** with any question, problem or idea. Please include your device (iPhone, iPad or Mac) and its software version.

## Common questions

**My events or reminders don't show up.**
Polite Badger needs access to Calendars and Reminders. On iPhone and iPad, go to Settings › Apps › Polite Badger and turn them on. On the Mac, use System Settings › Privacy & Security › Calendars and Reminders. In Polite Badger's Settings › Sources you can also check that the calendars and lists you want are turned on.

**My habits or notes don't appear on my other devices.**
They sync through your private iCloud. Make sure each device is signed in to the same Apple Account with iCloud turned on, and give it a few minutes. Pull down to refresh on iPhone and iPad, or press ⌘R on the Mac.

**The assistant or Scan Work Calendar isn't available.**
They use Apple Intelligence, which needs a supported device with Apple Intelligence turned on in Settings › Apple Intelligence & Siri. Scan Work Calendar is on iPhone and iPad.

**Scan Work Calendar missed a meeting or read a time wrong.**
Switch your work calendar to Day view, make sure the whole day and the date are on screen, and hold the phone square to the screen without glare. You can fix any meeting on the review screen before applying, and nothing is added until you tap Apply.

**How do I get my data out, or delete it?**
In Polite Badger, Settings › Your Data has Export All Data and options to delete your habits, notes, check-ins or diary entries.
